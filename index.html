Questions we hear most

Do I need to file returns if I earn only a salary?

Yes. Every person with a KRA PIN must file an annual return by 30 June, even when your employer already deducts PAYE. Nil and refund returns also need filing.

When must I register for VAT?

When your taxable supplies reach KES 5 million within 12 months, or you expect them to. You may also register voluntarily. We will confirm the current threshold during your intro call.

What happens if I missed a deadline?

KRA charges penalties and interest, but they can often be reduced through a waiver application. Reach out early and we will build a plan.

Can you work with me remotely?

Yes. Most clients across Kenya work with us entirely online through secure document sharing, phone and WhatsApp.
